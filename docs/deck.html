The problem

Onboarding dies quietly.

  • Every SDK team believes their quickstart works. Almost nobody re-tests it from a truly clean machine.
  • The next developer to hit your docs is increasingly an AI agent in a sandbox — no tribal knowledge, no second tab, no patience.
  • Today you learn your quickstart is broken from churned signups. That's the most expensive possible telemetry.

How it works
